# Bitcoin Merchant Laminated Paper (with QR Code)

## Overview
This laminated paper sign is a durable option for merchants to display their Bitcoin payment QR code. Laminated paper resists wear, making it suitable for indoor and semi-outdoor environments.

## Dimensions
- **Paper Size:** 23 cm (width) × 30 cm (height)  
- **QR Code Size:** 10 cm (width) × 10 cm (height)  

## Notes
- The QR code should be positioned prominently on the paper for easy visibility.  
- Laminating protects the paper from moisture, smudges, and frequent handling.  
- Suitable for display at cashier desks, on walls, or near POS terminals.
